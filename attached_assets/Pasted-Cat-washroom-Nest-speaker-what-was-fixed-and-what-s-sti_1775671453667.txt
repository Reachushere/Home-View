Cat washroom Nest speaker — what was fixed and what's still broken:

Background: The cat washroom has a reading system that plays course module readings aloud on a Nest speaker when the bathroom light turns on. It also displays text on a Fire tablet and Samsung TV. The Nest speaker stopped working — it would show "idle" and never actually play audio.

Root cause found: The Nest speaker is on a different VLAN than the Pi (172.24.1.204:5000) and Home Assistant (homeassistant.local:8123). The Nest can't reach either local address. But it CAN reach Nabu Casa cloud URLs (https://ec8ebfanqrqlsnmnggrdl4yzq2i8koah.ui.nabu.casa).

Fix deployed: All Nest speaker playback now goes through HA Cloud TTS via Nabu Casa. The flow is: call tts/speak to generate audio, get the tts_proxy URL from the response, convert homeassistant.local:8123 to the Nabu Casa cloud URL, stop the Nest (since tts/speak tried the unreachable local URL), then play the cloud URL via play_media. This is confirmed working — the Nest showed "playing" state.

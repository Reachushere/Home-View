The Information, Motivation, Behavioural Skills Model for Sexual and Reproductive Health Promotion In Module 8, Part 2, we saw how the information, motivation, behavioural skills theoretical model can be applied to adolescent sexuality education. Although it was initially developed and tested as a theoretical model to guide interventions targeting HIV AIDS sexual risk reduction, the IMB model can be used to guide a wide range of sexual health promotion and education activities . Whether we are creating and implementing programs to promote sexual health with individuals, groups, or communities, the IMB model provides a basic and well-tested conceptual framework for ensuring that our interventions are effective in reaching their objectives. First and foremost, sexual and reproductive health behaviors, whether they involve sexual function promotion, contraceptive utilization, STD HIV prevention, reproductive cancer screening, or sexual adaptations to aging, illness, or disability, represent sexualized behavioral events. Each of these sexual and reproductive health behaviors has acquired sexual meaning as a result of social ascription, individual learning, and the sexual and reproductive biology and physiology involved in all of the acts and adjustments in question. As sexualized acts, sexual and reproductive behaviors are all influenced by a common set of determinants of sexual behavior, including information, motivation, and behavioral skills factors, and all are potentially modifiable via common intervention pathways focusing on changing these factors in efforts to promote sexual and reproductive health (Fisher and Fisher, 1998, p. s 39-40). The IMB Model for Sexual Health Promotion Acquisition of Information Individual must learn information that is relevant to the practice of sexual and reproductive health behaviour. Development of Motivation Individual must become personally motivated and or socially motivated to practice sexual and reproductive health behaviour. Development of Behavioural Skills Individual must learn the actual behavioural skills to perform sexual and reproductive health behaviour. Although the necessity of including relevant information, motivation, and behavioural skills in sexual health promotion programs seems obvious, very few existing programs actually contain all three elements. In many cases, sexual health education programs have been exclusively information-based and, in particular, have neglected to provide specific behavioural skills training. Key Point The degree to which an individual is able to enact sexual health promoting behaviour will be dependant to a significant extent on their level of relevant information, motivation, and behavioural skills. Applying the IMB Model: Elicitation, Intervention, Evaluation Effectively applying the IMB model to a sexual health promotion intervention involves a basic three-step process: elicitation, intervention, and evaluation. In the elicitation phase, program planners assess or measure the target audience s information, motivation, and behavioural skills related to a specific set of sexual health behaviours. This can be done through focus groups, assessment interviews with clients, or questionnaires. In other words, conducting elicitation research will allow program planners to identify which aspects of information, motivation, and behavioural skills the target audience is lacking. Instead of creating programs based on our own assumptions or best guesses as to what the target audience needs, conducting elicitation research helps to ensure that an intervention will be relevant and have the desired impact. In the second step , the program is created and implemented based on the information, motivation, behavioural skills deficits weaknesses of the target audience. In the third step, the program is periodically assessed (again using focus groups, interviews, questionnaires, ect) to ensure that the program is successfully impacting on the information, motivation, and behavioural skills of the target audience. This information can be used to refine and improve the intervention. Key Point The comprehensive, systematic application of an IMB-based sexual health promotion program involves a three-step process of elicitation research, intervention, and evaluation. This program of information motivation behavioural skills and elicitation intervention evaluation will give you a basic structural framework for the delivery of effective sexual health promotion activities. Opportunities for Further Professional Development in the Area of Sexuality The field of sexology has advanced significantly in recent decades.
